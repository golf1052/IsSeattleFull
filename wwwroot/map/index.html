Candidates, not confirmed vacancy. Assessor classifications can lag construction and demolition. Low value does not mean unoccupied or buildable.

Nominal $1,000 valuations are normally excluded; the approved showroom-neighbor exception can override this. Cross-parcel improvement references remain excluded until independently verified.

Topography YES, Water Problems YES, and Restrictive Size Shape YES parcels are excluded by policy. These constraint flags do not prove occupancy or submersion.

Neighbor-based exceptions for size/shape and showroom classifications are marked for review; original attributes and accepted neighboring PINs remain visible.

Townhouse plats and construction-marked property names are excluded. These records are not a real-time construction status.

Bulk data is cross-checked against GIS: positive appraisals override zero/missing values, and GIS fills null attributes. Source tax years are shown separately from the comparison year; snapshots are cached.
